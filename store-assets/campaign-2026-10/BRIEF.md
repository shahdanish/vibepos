# SwiftTill — fresh Store media campaign

## Creative direction

Real shop work, clearly shown. A warm ivory canvas, deep navy typography, and SwiftTill green connect the existing register mark to a cleaner campaign. Each screenshot has one concrete benefit, a full readable application capture, and a small sample-data disclosure. Keep promotional text outside the app capture.

## New demonstration scenario

Use a new fictional retail shop: **Maple Corner Market**. Seed grocery and household products with fictional customers, no real contact information, realistic stock levels, and recent transactions. Show the actual Store Lite feature set for the main demo. Pro screens, if included, must have a visible Pro-subscription label. Do not show pharmacy or cloud-backup modules.

## Screenshot sequence

1. Checkout: Scan a grocery basket, show quantities, total, cash received and change. Caption: **Make every checkout feel simple.**
2. Products: Real product catalogue and stock quantities. Caption: **Keep your products organized.**
3. Overview: Low-stock alerts and top sellers. Caption: **See what needs restocking.**
4. Receipt preview: The fictional shop's identity. Caption: **Put your shop on every receipt.**
5. Sales report: Recent date range, revenue, cost and profit. Caption: **Understand the day behind the numbers.**
6. Customer ledger: A fictional customer's credit and repayment history. Caption: **Keep customer credit clear.**
7. Local backup: App-created demo database backup. Caption: **Keep a local copy of your records.**

Output: PNG, 2560 × 1440. Preserve original capture files separately. The UI inside each image must be a fresh, unaltered app capture; resize uniformly only. No invented buttons, figures, badges, reviews or interface screens.

## Video 1: shop workflow, about 50–65 seconds

- 0–3s: SwiftTill mark, “Your counter. Your stock. Your day.”
- 3–20s: Actual checkout: type/scan barcodes, add quantities, enter cash, show change. Keep the sequence understandable; do not promise measured checkout speed.
- 20–27s: Real branded receipt preview.
- 27–34s: Low-stock alerts and top sellers.
- 34–42s: Sales report, switch a date range.
- 42–51s: Customer credit ledger, select a fictional customer and show payments.
- 51–56s: Local backup.
- Final 4s: “Start with SwiftTill Lite” / “Available on Microsoft Store” / “Optional Pro subscriptions.”

Use captions, natural pacing, clean cuts and a consistent chapter strip. Demo data disclosure remains visible outside the app frame. Export MP4, H.264, 1920 × 1080, 30fps, AAC. Provide timed captions as a separate file. No stock footage presented as app footage.

## Video 2: short product introduction, about 20–30 seconds

Reuse the newly recorded scenes in a distinct short edit: checkout → stock alerts → sales report → customer credit → Store CTA. A short promo must retain actual app footage and the demo-data disclosure.
