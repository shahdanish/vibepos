Keep your shop moving with SwiftTill

SwiftTill brings checkout, inventory and customer credit into one Windows desktop app for grocery, convenience and general retail stores. Everyday billing works offline with local data, so a temporary internet outage does not stop your counter.

Start with free Lite

The Microsoft Store download is free. Lite includes retail billing, barcode and keyboard checkout, receipt printing, inventory, customer credit (khata), sales and profit reports, sale returns, cash-register shifts, and local database backup/restore. Lite supports up to two active users: an owner and a cashier. It is the normal free edition, not a time-limited Gumroad trial.

What your Pro purchase covers

The proposed US$1.99/month membership purchases a SwiftTill Pro software license for one Windows computer at one store while your paid subscription is active. It is not a purchase of the free installer, source code, or a Microsoft Store subscription.

Pro tools

• Wholesale billing

• Purchase entry, purchase returns, suppliers and demand orders

• Expense tracking

• Excel export

• More than two active staff users

• Custom roles and permissions

• Employee records and salary slips

Access also depends on the signed-in staff user's permissions. These features are confirmed in the current Store edition source; activation through Gumroad is still pending. Full pharmacy workflows and cloud backup are excluded from this offer.

Windows requirements

A 64-bit Windows 10 or Windows 11 desktop PC with access to Microsoft Store. Check the Store's compatibility information before installing. Internet access is needed for installation, updates and license activation/checks; a continuous connection is not needed for everyday billing. Printing requires a compatible receipt/A4 printer and its Windows driver. Barcode scanning requires a compatible scanner that enters barcodes as keyboard input. Hardware is sold separately.

FAQs

Is the Windows download free?

Yes. Install and use Lite from Microsoft Store for free; this proposed membership licenses the additional Pro tools.

Will a Gumroad payment unlock the current Store app?

No. Gumroad activation is not implemented yet. This draft is not for sale.

Should I also buy the Microsoft Store Pro subscription?

The current app uses Microsoft's in-app Pro subscription. That is a separate billing channel. Do not pay both for the same intended access.

Does it work offline?

Everyday billing and local data work offline. Installation and subscription validation require internet access. An offline validation grace period for Gumroad has not been verified.

Are screenshots customer testimonials?

No. Screenshots show the existing Store edition using demo data. They illustrate the interface and are not evidence of Gumroad activation.

Does SwiftTill process card payments or provide cloud backup?

This offer does not include payment processing or cloud backup. Use your card terminal separately and make local backups.

What if I need help moving to another PC?

Back up your shop database before uninstalling or moving. Email support to arrange a license transfer; a second simultaneous computer needs another license.
